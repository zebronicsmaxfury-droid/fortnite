FORTNITE OG - SEASON 1 BATTLE ROYALE (browser recreation)
=========================================================

A complete, single-player recreation of original (Season 1-2, late 2017) Fortnite Battle
Royale that runs entirely in your browser. No server, no downloads, no external assets:
every model, texture and sound is generated when the game loads.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------

1. Open "Fortnite OG.html" in Chrome or Edge (double-click it, or drag it into a browser tab).
2. Click CLICK TO LAUNCH (the island generates in about 3 seconds), then PLAY in the lobby.
3. Press F11 for full screen. Click the game to capture the mouse; Esc pauses.

A dedicated graphics card is recommended (the island is about 2 million triangles).


CONTROLS
--------

Every key below can be changed in SETTINGS (top bar of the lobby, or the pause menu): click
a binding and press the new key or mouse button. Mouse sensitivity, invert-Y, shadows,
grass and the GRAPHICS QUALITY selector live there too, and everything is saved in your
browser.

Move / sprint / crouch / jump ........ WASD / Shift / Ctrl / Space
Fire, swing pickaxe, place build ..... Left mouse
Aim down sights ...................... Right mouse (hold)
Reload ............................... R
Drop selected item .................... G (never drops the pickaxe)
Search chest / ammo box, pick up ..... E
Hotbar ............................... 1 = pickaxe, 2-6 = items, mouse wheel cycles
Build: wall / floor / stairs / roof .. F1 / F2 / F3 / F4
Build mode / rotate / material ....... Q / R / Right mouse (in build mode)
Map .................................. M
Jump from the bus, glider ............ Space (deploy early or retract while high enough)


WHAT IS IN THE GAME
-------------------

- Season 2 winter lobby with the Season 1-2 HUD tabs, level/XP and Free Pass panels,
  editable name, Solo / Duos / Squads selector, Locker with the eight default outfits.
  Progress (level, wins, eliminations) is saved in your browser.
- Spawn Island waiting area with 99 bots running around and the Battle Bus countdown
  (no weapons or ammo there - loot up on the main island after you drop).
- Battle Bus with balloon, random route across the island, "DOORS WILL OPEN IN 5s", jump
  only while over land, forced drop at the end of the route.
- Skydiving with contrails, glider auto-deploy near the ground, Space to deploy early or
  retract while high enough.
- The OG map: Anarchy Acres, Pleasant Park, Tomato Town, Wailing Woods (hedge maze),
  Loot Lake (island house), Lonely Lodge (tower), Dusty Depot (three warehouses),
  Retail Row, Greasy Grove (Durrr Burger), Salty Springs, Fatal Fields, Moisty Mire,
  Flush Factory (giant toilet), plus the motel, prison, racetrack, factories, container
  yard and scattered houses. Roads, rivers, hills, cliffs and forests.
- Loot: golden chests with glow and hum (chests now spawn much more often, including in
  open fields away from towns), ammo boxes, floor loot with rarity beams. Chests drop more
  ammo and often more than one ammo pool.
  Weapons: Assault Rifle (M16), SCAR, Tactical Shotgun, Pump Shotgun, Double Barrel
  Shotgun, Burst Assault Rifle, Submachine Gun, Tactical SMG, Suppressed SMG,
  Bolt-Action Sniper (projectile with drop), Semi-Auto Sniper (scoped, no bolt),
  Scoped Assault Rifle, Pistol, Revolver, Hand Cannon, Grenade Launcher (arcing
  projectile) and Rocket Launcher. Common -> Legendary rarities change damage and
  reload. Bandages, Med Kit, Small Shield, Shield Potion, Chug Jug.
- Graphics quality selector in SETTINGS: AUTO (adaptive), POTATO, LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH.
- 99 bots that always sprint, skydive to their chosen drop spots, loot chests, pick up
  better weapons, rotate with the storm, hear gunfire, react when shot, take cover with
  walls, ramp up, heal when hurt, and fight each other. Bots carry multiple guns and
  switch to the best one for the current range and ammo (shotgun up close, sniper at
  range), and swing a pickaxe when they run completely dry. Bot character models render
  much farther away than before. Duos/Squads give you bot teammates who follow you.
- Harvesting: pickaxe trees, rocks, cars and buildings for wood / brick / metal, with
  weak-spot critical circles.
- Building on the real grid: walls, floors, stairs, roof pyramids in wood / brick / metal,
  build-up HP, destructible by weapons and pickaxes. Every building on the map is made of
  the same destructible pieces.
- The Storm: nine shrinking phases with Season 2-style timings and 1/2/5/10 damage per
  second, purple wall, tinted sky, minimap and full-map circles, "storm is shrinking"
  warnings.
- 99 bots that skydive to their chosen drop spots, loot chests, pick up better weapons,
  rotate with the storm, hear gunfire, react when shot, take cover with walls, ramp up,
  heal when hurt, and fight each other. Duos/Squads give you bot teammates who follow you.
- Kill feed, damage numbers, hit markers, "#1 VICTORY ROYALE" and placement screens,
  compass, minimap with location names, materials panel and the F1-F5 build bar laid out
  like the Season 1 HUD.


KNOWN SIMPLIFICATIONS
---------------------

No structure editing, traps, down-but-not-out/revive, spectating or emotes. Placed builds
do not require support. Bots do not edit or use traps.
